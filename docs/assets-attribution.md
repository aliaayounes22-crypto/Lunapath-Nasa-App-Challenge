# LunaPath — Assets & Software Attribution

> **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**  
> **Challenge:** Lunar Landing Site Navigator  
> **Project:** LunaPath – Moon Landing Planner  

---

## 1. Imagery & 3D Visualization Assets

- **NASA CGI Moon Kit:**  
  Elevation and albedo color mapping derived from the NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS) Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) and Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA) public data products.  
  URL: [https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720)  
  License: NASA Public Domain.

- **USGS Astrogeology Science Center:**  
  Planetary nomenclature and polar coordinate references.  
  URL: [https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/)

- **LunaPath Mission Patch Logo:**  
  Custom team emblem created for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 featuring the Apollo-Artemis red vector, lunar crescent, and golden navigation reticle. Located at `/lunapath-logo.png`.
